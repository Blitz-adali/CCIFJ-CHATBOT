Conferences on the latest news from Japan, meetings with key 
personalities, discussions with experts on the Japanese market, CEO 
lunches, inter-chamber networking, seminars, Galas parties, company 
visits... More than a hundred events are organized by the CCI France-
Japan every year.
BUSINESS LEADERS FORUM
Since 2018, the French Japanese Business Leaders Forum has been a 
series of 10 thought leadership conferences. These events bring together 
industry experts to discuss trends, challenges, and opportunities in 
dedicated sectors, providing a networking platform to foster 
collaboration between France and Japan. Following these sectoral 
events, we culminate in our "French Japanese Business Summit" held in 
November.
BONJOUR FRANCE
Bonjour France hosts exceptional events in collaboration with its French 
and Japanese partners, showcasing the rich tapestry of the French way 
of life.
