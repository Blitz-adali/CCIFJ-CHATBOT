BUSINESS STRATEGY

In the morning, participants will explore why sustainable corporate growth
requires not only executives but also managers to have a strong understanding of
business strategy. Managers play a key role in translating strategy into concrete

actions and leading their teams to deliver results.

This session introduces core concepts of business strategy, key strategic thinking processes, and
widely used frameworks to analyse industries and competitive environments. It will help

participants broaden their perspective and strengthen their strategic decision-making skills.

CASE STUDY: Nitori & IKEA

Nitori has achieved long-term growth and market leadership in Japan through a highly integrated
manufacturing-logistics-retail model, despite g shrinking domestic market.

As it accelerates global expansion to reach 3,000 stores and JPY 3 trillion in sales by 2032, the key

question is whether it can sustain this success against global competitors like IKEA.

BRIDGING THE GAP: TURNING KNOWLEDGE INTO ACTION

In this session, we will explore the importance of o growth mindset and that
`knowing` and `doing` are not the same thing. Participants will learn how to create

psychological safety, the reasons why many business teams fail to achieve their

goals and tools to overcome challenges we all face in business and life.

Through interactive exercises and discussions, they will learn from each others` diverse experiences.

POWER & INFLUENCE

In the afternoon, participant will understand why achieving results within
organizations requires more than formal authority, such as position-based power,
budget control, or personnel allocation. It also requires the ability to engage others

and secure their cooperation. This session explores how influence can be exercised

effectively by understanding not only formal authority and rational direction but also

the mechanisms underlying human behavior, including emotions, values, and unconscious responses.

Key topics include:
* The importance of articulating a compelling vision of the desired future
« Approaches to motivoting people beyond relying on formal authority
* Fundamental steps for strategically building relationships and exercising influence

« Identifying one's own sources of power and strengthening personal influence

INTERCULTURAL COMMUNICATION

° INTERCULTURAL COMMUNICATION

Participants will develop essential communication skills to collaborate more
effectively with colleagues and clients from diverse cultural backgrounds and
work more efficiently in international contexts (including HQ-Subsidiaries

context). The session focuses on how differences in communication styles,

expectations, and ways of working can impact daily collaboration in multicultural
organizations. Through practical examples drawn from real workplace situations and guided
discussions, participants will better understand their own communication approach and how it may
be perceived by others. The emphasis is on practical application, helping participants adapt their
communication in meetings, decision-making, feedback, and cross-functional collaboration with

greater clarity, confidence, and effectiveness.

DIVERSITY MANAGEMENT

Harnessing the strengths of diverse talent has become a key factor in enhancing
long-term organisational competitiveness. This session explores the concept of
inclusion, not merely accepting diversity, but creating an environment in which

each individual's strengths can be fully utilized. It also examines the role of

PERSONAL MISSION & REFLECTION
November 2, 2026 | 9am - 5pm

In the first half of this module, participants will reflect on their three-month
learning journey, organizing insights gained through the program, identifying
practical applications to their work, and determining actions needed to
demonstrate leadership within their organizations moving forward.

In the latter half of the class, participants will explore the importance and impact

of kokorozashi (personal mission).

They will reflect on their aspirations as female leaders, what they wish to achieve, and share their
kokorozashi with other leaders in the class.

This exchange aims to foster meaningful networks that transcend organizational boundaries,

enabling mutual support among participants.

WELL-BEING MANAGEMENT

In this final session, we will draw on established theories from organizational
psychology, clinicol psychology, and developmental psychology to explore
mental health, work-life balance, boundary setting, and burnout prevention in
highly practical, actionable ways. With special attention to the unique

challenges often faced by women leaders, such as carrying invisible

responsibilities and balancing work and personal life, the session will be fully
interactive. Participants will have the opportunity to exchange ideas, experiences, and
perspectives in a comfortable setting, while discovering realistic strategies for protecting their
own wellbeing and continuing to perform at their best. They will leave with practical insights that

also contribute to building a healthier, more sustainable workplace culture for everyone.
