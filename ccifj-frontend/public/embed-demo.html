Embedding the concierge

This page is not part of the concierge. It only loads one script, the way a TYPO3 template would:

<script src="/embed.js" data-lang="fr" defer></script>

The button at the bottom right opens the chat. The language follows data-lang, then the page's <html lang>, then the browser.

A page can also open it from its own links: Poser une question au concierge.
